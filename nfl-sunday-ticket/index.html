NFL Sunday Ticket on YouTube TV is the streaming home of out-of-market NFL games. We place non-skippable :15 and :30 spots inside the live games, bought by the impression and targeted to your market.

The biggest afternoon of the week, targeted to your market.

Football Sundays draw the kind of audience most local advertisers can't afford to reach on broadcast TV. NFL Sunday Ticket on YouTube TV lets you buy that audience by the impression instead.

Your spot runs inside the live game, can't be skipped and reaches the demographics, locations and interests you choose.

We were first to market with NFL Sunday Ticket placements for Dahl Automotive, part of the strategy behind its growth from 6 stores to 16.

Questions about NFL Sunday Ticket.

Have a question that isn't here? Call (612) 599-8422 or send us a message.

What is NFL Sunday Ticket advertising?

In-game advertising during live out-of-market NFL games streamed on YouTube TV.

Are the ads skippable?

No. Both :15 and :30 spots are non-skippable.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your market and flight. On past campaigns, CPMs have started around $5. We'll build a custom plan with estimated reach and cost, free.

Can I target only my market?

Yes. Target by location, demographics, interests and online behavior.
