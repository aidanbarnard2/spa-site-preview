Advertising for private, family-owned business.

Founded in July 2015, Saint Paul Agency partners with privately held and family-owned businesses on advertising measured against real sales. We're a real team in downtown St. Paul, not a holding company.

Call (612) 599-8422

Who we work with.

We support more than 50 clients every month across automotive, retail, home services, healthcare, real estate, attractions and nonprofits. Some have one location, some operate in several states. All of them expect their advertising budget to work harder than a big-agency retainer.

We're a Google Premier Partner, a Meta Marketing Partner and the team that built FLOYD, our own demand-side platform for Connected TV, streaming audio and premium display.

  • Optimize

    We tighten your strategy, targeting and spend across every channel to cut waste.

  • Generate

    We put the plan to work across every channel, aimed at qualified leads, store visits and sales.

  • Grow

    We keep what works, cut what doesn't and keep refining the plan with you over time.

What you get with us.

  • Google Premier Partner

    We hold Google Premier Partner status for 2026, the top tier of Google's Partners program. Our buyers work closely with Google's U.S. teams and often get early access to new products.

  • Zero retainer fees

    Your budget goes to advertising, not agency overhead. We want to keep your business by doing good work, not by locking you into a long contract.

  • Our own DSP, FLOYD

    We built our own demand-side platform to place Connected TV, streaming audio and programmatic display with 300+ data partners.

  • National buying power

    We place over $50M in media a year across our clients. That volume helps us negotiate rates a single business often can't get alone.

  • Transparent reporting

    Clear reporting and invoicing, plus a live dashboard, so you can see what you're spending and what it's returning.

  • Results first

    We measure campaigns against business outcomes like leads, calls, store visits and sales, not just impressions.

What drives us.

  • Accountability

    We don't hide behind vanity metrics. We measure campaigns against your business outcomes.

  • Transparency

    Clear reporting and straightforward invoicing, so you can see where your money goes and what it returns.

  • Partnership

    We learn your goals and treat your success as our own. Our team works your numbers as hard as you do.

  • Innovation

    We built our own DSP instead of renting someone else's, and we stay ahead of platform changes so your budget works harder.
