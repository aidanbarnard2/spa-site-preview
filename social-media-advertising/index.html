Questions about Social Media Advertising.

Have a question that isn't here? Call (612) 599-8422 or send us a message.

Why advertise on social if we already post organically?

Organic builds your presence over time but reaches a small share of followers. Paid social reaches people who don't know you yet, with precise targeting.

How do you prevent wasted ad spend?

We refine custom and lookalike audiences continuously and remove underperforming segments, placements and creative.

Which platforms do you run ads on?

Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn and YouTube, all under one strategy.

Who makes the ads?

Our in-house creative team, so ads are built for each platform and stay consistent with your brand.
