How Borton Volvo became one of Volvo's top dealers in the country.

A family-owned Twin Cities Volvo dealership, founded around 1955, was losing local market share to its cross-town rival. We've been its agency of record since 2019.

The challenge.

  • Borton was losing local market share to its main cross-town competitor.
  • Volvo corporate was pressing the dealership to reverse slumping sales and service revenue.
  • Leadership had never worked with a marketing agency and was skeptical one could help.

What we did.

  • A new media mix

    We moved the dealership off a large legacy vendor and rebuilt its media plan from the ground up.

  • Google Ads

    We took over and optimized its Google search campaigns.

  • Premium radio

    We partnered with a well-known Twin Cities radio personality to build local trust.

  • Connected TV and paid social

    Targeted CTV and social campaigns drove higher-quality traffic to the website.
