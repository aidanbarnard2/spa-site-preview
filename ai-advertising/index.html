AI advertising on ChatGPT, Google AI and Copilot.

Someone will try to sell you an AI advertising package this year. We'll tell you what's actually live, what's still a pilot and what it costs to find out, then run the parts that make sense for your business.

Some of this is live today. Some is still a pilot.

AI advertising is paid placement inside AI-powered discovery tools: ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, and Microsoft Copilot. These tools read a whole question and its context, so an ad can appear while someone is comparing options and deciding.

A fair amount of what's being sold as AI advertising doesn't exist yet. Here's where each platform stands today.

  • LiveGoogle AI Overviews and AI ModeThrough your Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns
  • LiveMicrosoft Copilot and BingThrough Microsoft Advertising
  • Rolling outChatGPT adsAccess depends on location, category and inventory
  • Microsoft AdvertisingCertified, 2026

Every part of an AI campaign, planned and measured.

  • Google AI Overviews and AI Mode

    Your Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns can already appear in and around Google's AI results. The work is feed quality, creative, landing pages and bidding, and we're a Google Premier Partner.

  • ChatGPT advertising

    OpenAI is introducing ads gradually. We check whether you qualify today, what a test would cost and whether it's worth running yet. If the answer is not yet, we'll tell you.

  • Microsoft Copilot and Bing

    Copilot runs on the Bing index, and Microsoft ads already appear inside it. Volume is smaller, but the buyers are often better and the competition is thinner.

  • Getting recommended without an ad

    AI tools recommend businesses they understand. That comes down to site content, structured data, reviews and clarity, and it needs no media budget.

  • AI inside your current campaigns

    Performance Max, Advantage+ and automated bidding are AI, and they're spending your budget now. We review what the machine decided and step in when it's wrong.

  • Your own customer data

    As platforms automate targeting, the edge goes to better first-party data: customer lists, purchase history and site behavior feeding campaigns directly.

Questions about AI Advertising.

Have a question that isn't here? Call (612) 599-8422 or send us a message.

What is AI advertising?

Paid placement inside AI-powered discovery tools like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, and Microsoft Copilot. Ads can appear while someone is comparing options.

Can my business advertise on ChatGPT?

Possibly. OpenAI is rolling out ads in phases, and access depends on location, business category, eligibility and inventory. We'll check where you stand and tell you what a test would cost.

How is this different from regular Google Ads?

Less than it sounds. Your existing Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns can already appear in AI results. The questions are longer and more specific, so feed quality, landing pages and creative matter more.

How much should I spend to find out?

Enough to read the result. We start with an opportunity assessment and a test budget tied to something measurable. If your conversion tracking isn't working, we fix that first.
