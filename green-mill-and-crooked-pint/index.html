The challenge.

  • We had already started shifting budget from radio to Google Ads when COVID-19 arrived in March 2020.
  • Every dollar faced ROI scrutiny, leaving no room for pure branding spend.
  • The brands still had to open in new markets, including Glendale, Arizona, during the downturn.

What we did.

  • Digital first

    We moved nearly all advertising dollars to digital, mainly Google Ads.

  • Conquest remarketing

    Campaigns targeted guests of national competitors like Applebee's, Chili's and Buffalo Wild Wings.

  • Per-location budgets

    Each restaurant got its own budget, with web traffic, online orders and foot traffic tracked by location.
