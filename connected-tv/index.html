Your ad on the biggest screen in the house.

Connected TV puts your video ads on the streaming services people watch on their televisions. Unlike traditional TV, you target households, not time slots, and viewers can't skip or fast-forward.

We buy CTV directly through FLOYD, our own demand-side platform, so there's no media-company markup between your budget and the screen. On past campaigns, CPMs have run as low as $1.50. Your pricing depends on market, audience and inventory.

That includes live sports. Spots can run during March Madness, College GameDay and Sunday Night Football, alongside on-demand shows on Hulu, Peacock and Netflix.

Questions about Connected TV.

Have a question that isn't here? Call (612) 599-8422 or send us a message.

What is Connected TV advertising?

Video ads that run on streaming services watched on a TV, like Hulu, Peacock or YouTube TV. You can target viewers by demographics, location, interests and online behavior.

How is CTV different from traditional TV?

Traditional TV sells time slots. CTV targets households, so your spot reaches the people you choose. We also buy directly through our DSP, so there's no media-company markup.

Can viewers skip the ads?

No. Our CTV placements are non-skippable and play full-screen from start to finish.

What does CTV cost?

It depends on your markets, networks and audience. On past campaigns, CPMs have run as low as $1.50. We'll put estimated numbers for your market in a free plan.

Can each of my locations have its own campaign?

Yes. Multi-location campaigns can have separate geotargeting, audiences and reporting for each location.
