Questions about YouTube TV.

Have a question that isn't here? Call (612) 599-8422 or send us a message.

What is YouTube TV advertising?

Non-skippable :15 or :30 second video ads placed inside live sports and programming on YouTube TV.

What ad lengths are available?

:15 and :30 second spots. Both are non-skippable, and you can run multiple ads per event.

Which channels can I advertise on?

Sports, news and lifestyle networks, including NFL Sunday Ticket, ESPN, Fox News, HGTV and Hallmark.

How much do YouTube TV ads cost?

It depends on your market, networks and timing. On past campaigns, CPMs have run as low as $1.50 in premium programming and around $5 in NFL Sunday Ticket. We'll estimate yours in a free plan.

Can I target just my market?

Yes. Target by city, zip code or drive-time area, and run separate targeting for each location.
