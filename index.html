We built our own DSP. Meet FLOYD.

Most agencies rent programmatic buying from someone else and pass the markup along. In 2019 we built FLOYD, our own demand-side platform. It's named after Troy Alexander's grandfather, Floyd Sweet, an aviation pioneer who worked at NASA during the Apollo program.

FLOYD places your ads on Connected TV, streaming audio, digital billboards and premium websites using 300+ data partners like Experian, Nielsen, Mastercard and Oracle. Buying directly means no media company's markup stacked on top.

Common questions.

Have a question that isn't here? Call (612) 599-8422 or send us a message.

Do you charge a retainer?

No. We don't charge retainer fees, so your budget goes to media and creative. How we're paid is spelled out in your agreement before anything launches.

What does the free evaluation include?

We review your current advertising, flag the biggest opportunities and show you what we'd do differently. It's free and there's no obligation.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Privately held and family-owned businesses: auto dealer groups, restaurants, recreation, nonprofits, home services, retail and B2B. Some have one location, some operate in several states.

Do I need to be in Minnesota to work with you?

No. We're based in downtown St. Paul and work with businesses across the U.S.

Will I be locked into a long contract?

We don't require long-term commitments. The terms for your campaigns are spelled out in your agreement before anything launches.

What's the fastest way to reach you?

Call (612) 599-8422, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm Central. Form messages and emails go straight to our team.
