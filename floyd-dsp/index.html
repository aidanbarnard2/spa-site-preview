Why we built our own DSP.

Most agencies rent programmatic buying from someone else and pass the markup along. In July 2019 we built FLOYD so our clients could buy premium inventory directly.

FLOYD taps 300+ data partners, including Experian, Nielsen, Adobe, Comscore, Mastercard, Visa and Oracle, and buys across 34+ exchanges like Google Ad Exchange, OpenX, SpotX and Telaria.

On past prime-time, non-skippable campaigns, our CPMs have run 30% to 50% below industry averages. Your pricing depends on market, audience and inventory.

The name has roots.

FLOYD is named after Troy Alexander's grandfather, Floyd Sweet (1914–2006).

He earned U.S. glider pilot certificate No. 45 in 1931, at 17, and helped lead military glider programs during World War II. He joined NASA in 1961 and served there during the Apollo program. He was inducted into the Soaring Hall of Fame in 1963 and named an Elder Statesman of Aviation by the National Aeronautic Association in 1988.

He spent his career reading data other people missed. The platform that carries his name does the same thing with ad exchanges instead of airspace.

Questions about FLOYD DSP.

Have a question that isn't here? Call (612) 599-8422 or send us a message.

What is a DSP?

A demand-side platform is software that buys digital ad space automatically, in real time, across many ad exchanges.

How is FLOYD different from other DSPs?

We own and run it. Most agencies resell someone else's platform and add a markup.

Where can FLOYD place my ads?

Connected TV, streaming audio and podcasts, digital billboards, premium websites and apps on mobile, tablet and gaming consoles.

Why buy through FLOYD?

Fewer middlemen. Buying directly avoids the markups media companies often add on top.

How is brand safety handled?

Category exclusions, allowlists and private marketplace deals keep your ads in brand-safe places.

Is FLOYD a fit for a smaller business?

We built it so privately held businesses can buy the same kinds of inventory national brands buy. Whether it fits your budget is something we'll look at in your free evaluation.
