Questions about Lead Generation.

Have a question that isn't here? Call (612) 599-8422 or send us a message.

What channels do you use for lead generation?

Whatever channels your buyers actually use: paid search on Google and Microsoft, Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn lead ads, and retargeting. One team runs all of it.

How do you measure lead quality, not just quantity?

Leads can flow into your CRM with their channel, campaign, keyword and ad attached. That lets us connect spend to closed revenue and track cost per acquisition, not just cost per lead.

How long until we see results?

It varies by business and market. First leads often come in within a few weeks of launch, and a pipeline you can plan around usually takes a few months of testing.

Do you work with our existing CRM?

In most cases, yes. We connect leads to the CRM you already use, with source details attached.

What's the difference between inbound and outbound lead generation?

Outbound goes looking for customers with cold calls and emails. Inbound makes you easy to find when buyers are already looking. Most of what we build is inbound.

What makes a good landing page?

One job. A good landing page asks the visitor to take one action, which is why we build dedicated pages for campaigns instead of sending traffic to a homepage.
